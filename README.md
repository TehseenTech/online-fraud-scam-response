# Online Financial Fraud & Scam Response

A Pakistan-focused, evidence-first guide for responding to online payment fraud, impersonation scams, phishing, and unauthorized transactions.

## If the incident is happening now

- Stop further payments and do not send any additional fees, OTPs, PINs, or recovery codes.
- Contact the bank, wallet, card issuer, or payment service through its official channel; ask whether the transfer can be held, recalled, or disputed.
- Secure the email and phone accounts linked to financial services; change passwords from a trusted device and enable multi-factor authentication.
- Write down the transaction ID, amount, date, recipient details, platform, and the exact sequence of events.
- Report promptly to the relevant financial provider and Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA).

## Evidence checklist

- Original chats, emails, voice notes, call logs, advertisements, and profile links
- Payment receipts, transaction IDs, account statements, beneficiary details, and dispute numbers
- Full URLs and screenshots that include the address bar, date, and time
- A chronological incident timeline; preserve originals and work from copies

## What not to do

- Do not pay a person who promises guaranteed fund recovery.
- Do not confront the suspected fraudster in a way that risks deletion of evidence.
- Do not publish bank statements, CNIC details, phone numbers, or private chats in a public GitHub issue.

## Simple incident timeline

| Date and time | Time zone | Event | Account or URL | Evidence file | Action taken | Reference number |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM | PKT / UTC+5 | What happened | Identifier | File name | Report or containment step | Ticket / complaint ID |

Keep facts separate from assumptions. Record times exactly as displayed and note the time zone. Preserve original files; use copies for review or redaction.

## Reporting in Pakistan

For cybercrime complaints, use the official [NCCIA complaint portal](https://complaint.nccia.gov.pk/) or the current reporting methods listed on the [NCCIA website](https://www.nccia.gov.pk/). For immediate physical danger, contact local emergency services. For urgent financial loss, contact the relevant bank, wallet, card issuer, or payment provider first.

## Privacy and repository safety

This is a public repository. **Do not open an issue containing victim names, CNIC or passport numbers, phone numbers, financial details, credentials, private media, chat exports, case files, or other evidence.** Use official private reporting channels. Redact examples before sharing them for documentation improvements.
